Brand Mission

We are always committed to the mission of creating happy hot pot time and spreading healthy hot pot culture to foodies all over the world through our products and innovative services. As a global restaurant chain rooted in integrity, Haidilao strives to provide customers with thoughtful service and ensure food that is healthier, safer, and more nutritious. We continuously enhance food quality and safety to uphold the highest standards for our customers

Brand
Vision

Communication is essential to building trust among people and Chinese hot pot is the perfect way to foster social connections at the dinner table. Haidilao is dedicated to encouraging meaningful conversations, bringing people together over meals, and cultivating a dining culture that resonates with young people worldwide.

Signature Services

At Haidilao, customer experience is our top priority. We are committed to delivering innovative and enjoyable services, which many are inspired by the creativity of our staff. Their thoughtful and customized services turn every meal into a happy experience.

Training And Development

Super Hi Haidilao has established its own Learning & Development Center to support the strategic growth of the company and its staff. This initiative helps to preserve and pass down our corporate culture, define the essence of best business practices, and equip employees with practical skills. Guided by the motto "Hard work can change destiny, and wisdom makes dreams come true," we have launched specialized training programs for various restaurant roles. Not only do we offer a diverse range of courses, including cultural systems, vocational skills, and hands-on activities, but we also provide employees with flexible learning opportunities by offering both online and offline training platforms for them to access anytime, anywhere. We want to strive for the sustainable development of our enterprise and foster a culture of lifelong learning.
